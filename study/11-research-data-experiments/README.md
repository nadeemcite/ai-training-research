# Topic 11 — Research skill: data experiments (diversity, order, curriculum)

**Pichle topic se link:** Topic 10 me pretraining chalana seekha. Ab asli **research** shuru hoti hai: "kaunsa data, kis order me, kitna?" Ye sawaal poochna, sahi experiment banana, aur result ko **imaandaari se** judge karna. Video ke hisaab se aaj ke AI researcher ka yahi main kaam hai.

> Video (01:30): *"Your job as AI researcher is just to have high-level idea which experiments to do."*

**Time:** ~55 min (padhai) + ~20 min (lab). Ye course ka sabse important "soch" wala topic hai.
